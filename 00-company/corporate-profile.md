## Company overview (boilerplate)

GAMA-1 Technologies delivers mission-focused IT services to federal civilian and defense agencies. Our teams modernize legacy systems, secure enterprise environments, and turn data into decisions. We operate under ITIL 4, ISO 20000, ISO 27001, and ISO 9001 management systems. We apply NIST SP 800-53 controls and Zero Trust principles in every engagement. Our NOAA work proves we can process, secure, and deliver scientific data at operational scale. Agencies choose GAMA-1 for low-risk transition, measurable outcomes, and disciplined service management.
