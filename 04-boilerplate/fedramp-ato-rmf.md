# Boilerplate: ATO, RMF, and FedRAMP

[AGENCY] needs systems that reach authorization fast and stay authorized. GAMA-1 executes all seven steps of the NIST Risk Management Framework under SP 800-37 Rev 2. These steps are prepare, categorize, select, implement, assess, authorize, and monitor.

We categorize each system under FIPS 199 and FIPS 200. We tailor SP 800-53 Rev 5 baselines to mission risk. We write control implementation statements that assessors can test. We assess with SP 800-53A procedures and track every weakness in a POA&M. We author packages in OSCAL where the agency accepts machine-readable artifacts.

For cloud services, we inherit controls from FedRAMP-authorized providers. This cuts the control burden on [AGENCY]. We align with OMB M-24-15 and the FedRAMP 20x approach of automated Key Security Indicators.

After authorization, we operate continuous monitoring under SP 800-137. We report status to CDM dashboards. We close POA&M items on schedule and keep the authorizing official informed.

The result is a shorter path to ATO, fewer findings, and an authorization that stays current.
