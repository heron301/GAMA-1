# Boilerplate: Quality Management

GAMA-1 manages quality under an ISO 9001 quality management system. [VERIFY certification status in `00-company/certifications-register.md` before you claim "certified."]

Our Quality Control Plan defines a standard, a measure, and an owner for each deliverable. We inspect deliverables before release to [AGENCY]. We track defects to root cause and fix the process, not just the product. We align our metrics with the [AGENCY] Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan. We report quality results to the COR each month.

The result is deliverables that meet the standard the first time, with less government review effort.
