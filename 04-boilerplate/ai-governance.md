# Boilerplate: Responsible AI Adoption

[AGENCY] wants AI that saves staff time without new mission risk. GAMA-1 starts each AI effort with a clear use case and a measured baseline.

We assess data quality and sensitivity before we build. We select the deployment model that fits the data: on-premises, cloud, or hybrid. We manage risk under the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and its Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1). We follow current OMB AI guidance and document each use case for the agency AI inventory.

We keep a human in the loop for every consequential decision. We test models for accuracy, bias, and drift before and after release. We log model inputs and outputs for audit.

The result for [AGENCY] is measurable productivity gain with governed, explainable risk.
