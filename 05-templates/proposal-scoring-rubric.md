# Proposal Scoring Rubric

Score each section 1 to 100 on each factor. Record the strengths, weaknesses, and fixes an evaluator would note.

| Factor | What earns a high score |
|---|---|
| Customer understanding | Restates the mission in agency words. Names real pain points and priorities. |
| Technical approach | Addresses every requirement. Shows how, not just what. Names standards. |
| Innovation | Offers credible automation or AI/ML with a measured benefit. |
| Security | Grounds the approach in RMF, NIST SP 800-53, Zero Trust, and FedRAMP. |
| Risk | Names risks and mitigations. Shows low-risk transition. |
| Programmatic / management | Clear organization, roles, key personnel, QC, and reporting. |
| Compliance | Follows Section L structure, page limits, and format exactly. |
| Readability | Active voice. Sentences of 20 words or fewer. Labeled figures and tables. |
