# Systems Engineering & Administration

## Outcome

GAMA-1 keeps infrastructure available, secure, and ready for growth. Agencies gain stable operations and a clear view of system health.

## Approach

We design and operate network, server, storage, and telecom infrastructure. We run Network Operations Center and Security Operations Center functions. We apply configuration baselines and patch under NIST SP 800-40. We monitor capacity and plan upgrades before demand exceeds supply. We document architecture so the agency keeps full knowledge of its systems. We support classified environments under ICD 503 where required.

## Standards and frameworks

| Area | Standard |
|---|---|
| Engineering | ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288, INCOSE practice |
| Operations | ITIL 4, ISO 20000 |
| Security | NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5, DISA STIGs, ICD 503 |
| Patch management | NIST SP 800-40 Rev 4 |
