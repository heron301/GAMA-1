# Cybersecurity & Compliance

## Outcome

GAMA-1 shortens time to ATO and keeps authorizations current. Agencies gain a defensible risk posture, fewer open POA&M items, and continuous visibility of threats.

## Approach

We execute the full NIST Risk Management Framework under SP 800-37. We categorize systems under FIPS 199 and select baselines under SP 800-53 Rev 5. We assess controls with SP 800-53A procedures. We author SSPs, SARs, and POA&Ms in OSCAL where the agency supports it. We automate evidence collection to support FedRAMP 20x Key Security Indicators. We design Zero Trust architecture under SP 800-207 and OMB M-22-09. We operate continuous monitoring under SP 800-137 and feed CDM dashboards. We run incident response under SP 800-61 Rev 3. We manage identity under HSPD-12 and FICAM with phishing-resistant MFA.

## Standards and frameworks

| Area | Standard |
|---|---|
| Governance | FISMA 2014, OMB A-130 Appendix I, annual OMB FISMA guidance (e.g., M-24-04) |
| Risk | NIST SP 800-30, 800-37, 800-39 |
| Controls | NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5, 800-53A, 800-53B, 800-171 |
| Cloud | FedRAMP (OMB M-24-15), OSCAL, FedRAMP 20x |
| Architecture | NIST SP 800-207, CISA ZT Maturity Model v2.0, TIC 3.0 |
| Operations | NIST SP 800-61 Rev 3, 800-137, 800-40, OMB M-21-31 logging |
| Crypto / identity | FIPS 140-3, HSPD-12, FICAM |
| Supply chain | NIST SP 800-161, 800-218 |
| DoD | CMMC Level 2, DoD Zero Trust Strategy |
