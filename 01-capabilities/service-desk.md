# Service Desk / Help Desk Operations

## Outcome

GAMA-1 restores service fast and prevents repeat incidents. Agencies gain higher first-contact resolution, shorter outages, and satisfied users.

## Approach

We operate Tier I through Tier III support, 24x7 where the mission requires it. We run all practices under ITIL 4 and an ISO 20000 service management system. We use ServiceNow for incident, request, problem, change, and knowledge management. We measure performance against agreed SLAs and publish results each month. We use problem management to remove root causes, not just symptoms. We build self-service knowledge so users solve common issues without a ticket.
