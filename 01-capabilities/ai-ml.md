# AI / ML Integration

## Outcome

GAMA-1 delivers trustworthy AI that removes manual work and speeds decisions. Agencies gain measurable productivity with governed, explainable risk.

## Approach

We start from a mission use case and a measurable baseline. We assess data readiness before we build. We design on-premises, cloud, or hybrid AI infrastructure to fit data sensitivity. We apply generative AI to federal workflows such as document processing, search, and drafting. We keep a human in the loop for consequential decisions. We document each use case for the agency AI inventory. We manage risk under the NIST AI RMF and current OMB AI guidance.

## Standards and frameworks

| Area | Standard |
|---|---|
| Risk | NIST AI RMF 1.0, NIST AI 600-1 (GenAI profile) |
| Policy | Current OMB AI memoranda (e.g., M-25-21, M-25-22) [VERIFY current] |
| Security | NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5, NIST SP 800-218A |
| Data | Federal Data Strategy |
