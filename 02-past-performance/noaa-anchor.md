## Mission context

NOAA works to understand and predict changes in climate, weather, ocean, and coasts. It shares that knowledge with others. It conserves and manages coastal and marine ecosystems and resources. GAMA-1 supports this mission with reliable scientific data products.

## Scope

GAMA-1 processes scientific data across the full lifecycle, from Level 0 raw data to Level 2b+ and higher products. The team operates automated pipelines for ingest, quality control, processing, and distribution. The team secures the environment under NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 and maintains its ATO.
